      Monthly profit
      
      
      Upload the monthly Meesho payment zip (or the .xlsx inside it), enter the month's packing cost and SKU prices, and get the profit. The file is read on this phone and is not uploaded. The result is saved to your Google Sheet.
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Month and packing
      
      
      Profit counts every line Meesho paid in the chosen month (by Payment Date). Packing changes every month, so enter it each time.
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      Cost price per SKU
      
      
      Unit cost including GST, one price for each category of your SKU list (Settings, Manage SKUs). Every SKU in the category gets that price. A SKU that is not in your list is asked on its own. Prices from your last saved month are filled in, so check them. An order of quantity 2 costs 2 × the unit price.
      
      
      Paste a price list instead
        
      One per line, price at the end. Use a category name or a SKU, for example 1 Mtr pack of 10 400 or PRINT_1MT_10PC 400

      How profit is calculated
        
      Cash basis: every line in Meesho's Order Payments sheet whose Payment Date is in the month. Cost = unit cost × quantity.
      
        
      LineProfit
      
        
      Monthly profit = sum of line profits − ads cost (Ads Cost sheet, including GST, deducted once). GST payable, TCS/TDS credits and fixed expenses are not included.

      Report fraud
      
    
      Type or paste an order ID. The tool finds the customer in your master sheet. Add it to the fraud list with a reason, and every new label is checked against the list.
